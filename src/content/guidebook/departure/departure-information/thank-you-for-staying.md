## 🌟 We love online reviews

If you have a moment, please leave us a review on our Facebook page: Beach Retreat Kommetjie.

Vrbo and Airbnb auto sends you the review request also, so all you need to do is click the "leave a review" button, give us 5 stars(!), and write a sentence or two about what you liked the best during your stay.

Was it the the location? Or the swimming pool? Or maybe it was our sustainable initiatives?

If you don't feel able to leave us 5 stars, please don't hesitate to contact us first to let us know why - you are key to helping our next guests to have a wonderful time.
