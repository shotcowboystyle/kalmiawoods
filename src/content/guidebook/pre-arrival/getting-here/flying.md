The closest international airport is Cape Town International Airport.

We've mapped out driving directions from the airport to Beach Retreat. Find them here.

Unfortunately, there are very limited public transport links between the airport and Kommetjie (the price we pay for such a quiet, secluded spot!). We'd highly recommend renting a car for the time you're with us. There are a range of car hire services at the airport:

- AVIS Rent A Car
- Hertz Rent A Car
- Woodford Car Hire
- Sixt
- Capital Car Hire
