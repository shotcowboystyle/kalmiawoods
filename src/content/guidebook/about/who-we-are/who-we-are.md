## Hi, I'm Jane

I own Beach Retreat, a restful hideaway tucked into the beautiful South African coastline.

I grew up in Cape Town, and I've lived in this region for most of my life. I know it like the back of my hand, and I can't wait to share it with you.

From gorgeous vineyards and beaches, to the bustling hub of Cape Town, there's so much for you to discover.

I've packed as much insight as I can into this guidebook, but if there's anything missing or confusing, don't hesitate to get in touch!

You can reach me at jane.cartwright@touchstay.com.

## Meet the team

I run Beach Retreat with my partner, Jamie, who loves it as much as I do. She moved to the Western Cape later in life, after travelling all over the world. When she arrived here, she found she just didn't want to leave!
