## Why do we do what we do

We're driven by a passion for the Western Cape of South Africa and a desire to share this stunning spot with others. From the culture and excitement of Cape Town, awe-inspiring views along the coastal road, to the cosy bolthole of Beach Retreat itself, we love introducing our guests to our home.

## Sustainable tourism

Given that we love the Western Cape so much, we understand the importance of running our holiday rental in a way that minimizes our impact on the surrounding environment.

Take a look at our Making a Difference section in this guidebook to discover how we're supporting our local community and reducing our environmental impact.
