When you first access this guidebook, you'll notice a prompt to add it to your home screen. This is highly recommended. It makes your guidebook faster, easier to access, and allows you to access the guidebook if you're somewhere without reception.

Following the prompt will add an app icon to your phone. Once it's added to your phone, it'll feel just like any other app - but without you needing to download anything or sign in! Magic 🪄
