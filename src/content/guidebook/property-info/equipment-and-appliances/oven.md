To preheat the oven, turn the dial to the far left to the programme you need. If you're turning clockwise, you'll reach:

- the standard setting on the second turn of the dial
- the fan setting on the third turn of the dial
- the grill setting on the fifth turn of the dial

Then, turn the dial to the right to the temperature you need. You'll find more detailed instructions in the manual in the cutlery drawer.

NB. we recommend preheating the oven for at least 15 minutes before you want to use it.
