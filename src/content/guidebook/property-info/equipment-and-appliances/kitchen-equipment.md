The kitchen is one of the places we hang out the most. The kids love the sit-up bar and it often becomes the place where we all end up congregating and chatting.

Because we love this space to much, you can be sure that it's properly kitted out! You'll find, amongst other things:

- a fridge
- separate freezer
- dishwasher
- microwave
- gas fired cook top
- double oven
- wine cooler
- high quality cookware

We've included a starter supply of dishwasher tablets and washing up liquid. You'll also find tea towels in draw to the right of the sink.

Should you have any questions before (or during) your stay please let us know!
