We leave the microwave on its highest power setting. To adjust the time, simply turn the dial until the display shows the number of seconds/minutes you need. Press "Start" and you're good to go.

If you want to use a specific programme (e.g. defrosting), you can find instructions in the manual on top of the microwave.
