The aircon can be controlled using the remote that's on the wall in the living room near the door to the kitchen.

We always maintain the temperature at around 71F - 73F, and it shouldn't be necessary to adjust it beyond that range. However, if you do need to, you can simply press the up or down arrow on the panel.

Please ensure all doors and windows are closed whilst it is running otherwise it will not operate efficiently.
