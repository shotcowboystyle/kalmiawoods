## A warm welcome to you

In this guide, we'll provide you with all the information you need for your stay with us, from getting the most from your time in Cape Town, all the way through to your checkout.

In this easy-to-navigate web app, you'll find everything you need to know about:

how to get into the property
what you need to pack
gorgeous spots for a braai
how to access the Wi-Fi
And that's just the tip of the iceberg!

Hint for Touch Stay customers: a welcome video can add some personal warmth to this section, and help your guests to put a face to a name! Jayne Hancock, of Fields Lodge B&B, has done a brilliant job of welcoming guests to her guide.

## Before you go any further

### Add this guidebook app to your phone

If you haven't already followed the prompt to add this app to your phone, we recommend that you do! It just takes a few seconds, and it will allow you to find this guidebook in the future by tapping the app icon on your phone screen (like a normal app!). It will also allow you to access the guide when there's no phone reception.

Just open the book on your phone and follow the prompt. It'll miraculously add the app to your home screen!

### Print or save a PDF

If you'd like to print some of the pages ahead of travelling, or if you'd like to save a PDF to your device, just tap the print button above. It won't print right away but it will create a PDF that you can save or print.

### And now, relax

That's it! Please relax, unwind, have fun, and enjoy your stay! We're really happy you're here :)
