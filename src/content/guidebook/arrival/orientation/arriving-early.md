Many guests arrive before the designated check-in time. The good news is that there's plenty to do. We often suggest that guests visit the grocery store and do a small shop. That will give you more time to relax once you've checked in.

There are also some great restaurants for lunch - so why not relax and enjoy some of the local food? You can also check out our Day Trips section.
