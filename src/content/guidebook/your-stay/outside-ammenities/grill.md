We provide a BBQ grill on the deck. It's simple to use - just press the automatic starter on the grill!

Please remember to turn the gas on! There is also a set of BBQ tools in the kitchen.

If the propane runs out there is a spare tank in the garage. Please clean the BBQ after use and keep it covered when not in use to prevent wear and tear.
