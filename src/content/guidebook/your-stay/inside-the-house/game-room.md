On the lower level of the house, you'll find a games room complete with a pool table, Xbox 360, table football, and a darts board.

You'll also find a number of board and card games in the cupboard, and books on the shelves. Feel free to use any of these, please just make sure they find their way back to the right spot before you leave.

If you want to use the Xbox please ensure the TV source is switched to HDMI 1 which you do from the "source" button on the TV remote control.
